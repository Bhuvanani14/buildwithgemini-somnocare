      record_voice_over Oropharyngeal (Throat) OSA Protocol

      Supportive Therapy (Not a Standalone Cure):

      Clinical trials (Guimarães et al., AJRCCM) suggest myofunctional throat exercises may reduce snoring and mild-to-moderate OSA severity by toning upper airway dilator muscles:

      1. Tongue Slide (20 reps): Press tongue tip against hard palate and slide backward along roof of mouth.
      2. Palatal Tongue Suction (20 reps): Suck entire tongue upward against palate, holding 5 seconds.
      3. Soft Palate Lift (20 reps): Elevate uvula and soft palate while intermittently saying "Ahhh".
      4. Buccal Cheek Resistance (10/side): Press finger gently against inner cheek while contracting buccinator muscle.
